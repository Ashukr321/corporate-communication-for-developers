# Standup (Daily Scrum)

Keep it to **30–60 seconds**. Three parts:

1. **Yesterday** — what you completed
2. **Today** — what you'll work on
3. **Blockers** — anything stopping you

## Example
> "Yesterday I completed the login form validation and raised the PR.
> Today I'll address review comments and start on the forgot-password flow.
> No blockers as of now."

## With a blocker
> "I'm blocked on the payments API — the staging endpoint is returning 500s. I've reached out to the backend team; meanwhile I'll work on the UI with mock data."

## Don'ts
- Don't go into deep technical detail — "let's take it offline".
- Don't say "same as yesterday" — say what progressed.
