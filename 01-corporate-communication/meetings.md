# Meetings

## Joining & starting
- "Hi everyone, can you hear me?"
- "Shall we get started?"
- "Let's wait a couple of minutes for others to join."

## Speaking up
- "Can I add something here?"
- "Sorry to interrupt — quick question on that point."
- "Building on what Ankit said..."

## Clarifying
- "Just to make sure I understood correctly — you mean...?"
- "Could you share your screen?"
- "Could you repeat that? You broke up a bit."

## Disagreeing politely
- "I see your point, but I have a slightly different view."
- "Have we considered the trade-off with...?"
- "That could work. One concern I have is..."

## Wrapping up
- "To summarize, the action items are..."
- "I'll send out the notes after the call."
- "Thanks everyone, that's all from my side."

## Etiquette
- Mute when not speaking.
- Keep camera on for small team calls if that's the team norm.
- Share an agenda for meetings you organize.
- Send notes + action items within the same day.
