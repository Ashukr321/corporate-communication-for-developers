# Escalation

**Escalating** = raising an issue to someone with more authority when it can't be solved at your level. It's normal — not complaining.

## When to escalate
- A blocker isn't resolved after reasonable follow-ups.
- A deadline is at risk and you need a decision.
- A production issue is beyond your access/knowledge.
- Conflicting priorities from two people.

## Phrases
- "I've followed up twice without a response — could you help escalate?"
- "Flagging this to you since it may impact the release date."
- "I'd like to escalate this for visibility."
- "I need a decision on X by tomorrow to stay on track."
- "Raising this to the on-call lead."

## Template
> "Hi [Manager], flagging an issue for visibility.
> **Issue:** Staging DB access still pending (requested Mon).
> **Impact:** Blocks QA for the release on Friday.
> **Tried:** Followed up with DevOps on Tue and Wed.
> **Ask:** Could you help expedite with the DevOps lead?"

## Tip
Escalate the **problem**, not the **person**. Stick to facts, impact, and the ask.
