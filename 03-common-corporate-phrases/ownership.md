# Ownership

Taking ownership = being the person who makes sure something gets done, not necessarily doing all of it yourself.

## Phrases
- "I'll take this up."
- "I'll own this end to end."
- "Assign it to me, I'll pick it up after the current ticket."
- "I'll drive this and keep everyone posted."
- "That's on me — I missed it. Here's the fix."
- "I'll follow up with the backend team and close the loop."

## Ownership vs not
| Instead of | Say |
|------------|-----|
| "Not my code." | "I'm not familiar with this module, but I'll take a look." |
| "Someone should fix this." | "I've raised a ticket and can pick it up." |
| "QA didn't catch it." | "We missed this case — I'll add a test for it." |

## Related terms
- **Owner / DRI** (Directly Responsible Individual) — the one person accountable.
- **Point of contact (POC)** — who to reach out to.
- **Accountable** — answerable for the outcome.
