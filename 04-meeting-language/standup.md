# Standup — Meeting Language

## Starting your update
- "From my side..."
- "Yesterday I worked on..."
- "I was able to complete..."

## Today's plan
- "Today I'll be picking up..."
- "I'll continue with..."
- "Plan for today is to address review comments and move the ticket to QA."

## Blockers
- "No blockers as of now."
- "I have one blocker — ..."
- "I need some help from @Aman on the DB migration; will sync after standup."

## Parking deep discussions
- "Let's take this offline."
- "Can we discuss this after standup?"
- "I'll set up a separate call for this."

## Example
> "From my side, yesterday I completed the order history API and raised the PR. Today I'll address review comments and start the pagination changes. No blockers."
