# Sprint Planning

Goal: decide what the team commits to in the next sprint.

## Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Sprint | Fixed work period (usually 2 weeks) |
| Sprint goal | The one outcome the sprint aims for |
| Capacity | How much work the team can take (accounting for leaves) |
| Velocity | Average story points completed per sprint |
| Commitment | Work the team agrees to deliver |
| Carry-over / Spillover | Unfinished work moved to next sprint |

## Phrases
- "I'm OOO for two days this sprint, so my capacity is lower."
- "I can take up the search ticket."
- "I think this is too much for one sprint."
- "Can we carry over JIRA-210 from last sprint?"
- "What's the sprint goal?"
- "This depends on the backend, so let's plan it for the second week."
