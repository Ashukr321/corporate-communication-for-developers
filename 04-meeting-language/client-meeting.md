# Client Meetings

Be clear, confident, and non-technical unless the client is technical.

## Opening
- "Thanks for joining. Today we'll walk you through the progress and next steps."
- "Here's the agenda for today..."

## Demo
- "Let me share my screen and walk you through the feature."
- "This is currently on staging, so some data is dummy data."
- "Please feel free to stop me if you have questions."

## Handling questions you can't answer
- "That's a great question. Let me confirm with the team and get back to you by tomorrow."
- "I'd like to check the details before committing to a date."

## Handling scope changes
- "That's a valid request. It's outside the current scope, so we'll evaluate the effort and share an estimate."
- "We can add this in the next phase."

## Handling bad news
- "We've identified a delay in X due to Y. The revised date is Z, and here's what we're doing to mitigate it."

## Closing
- "To summarize, the next steps are..."
- "We'll share the meeting notes by EOD."

## Avoid
- Committing to dates without checking with the team.
- Blaming internal teams or individuals.
- Too much jargon ("we refactored the reducer") — say "we improved performance".
