# Retrospective (Retro)

Goal: improve how the team works. Blameless — focus on process, not people.

## Common formats
- **Went well / Didn't go well / Action items**
- **Start / Stop / Continue**
- **Mad / Sad / Glad**

## Phrases — what went well
- "Shout-out to @Meera for helping with the release."
- "Code reviews were much faster this sprint."
- "Pairing on the migration worked really well."

## Phrases — what didn't go well
- "Requirements changed mid-sprint, which caused rework."
- "We had a lot of context switching due to prod issues."
- "PRs were waiting for review for 2–3 days."

## Phrases — action items
- "Can we agree on a 24-hour PR review SLA?"
- "Let's finalize designs before the sprint starts."
- "I'll take the action item to document the deploy steps."

## Tip
Say "**we**" not "**you**": "We missed the edge case" instead of "You didn't test it".
